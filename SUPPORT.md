# Supporting GhostDLL

GhostDLL is an independent spare-time technical experiment.

If voluntary financial support is enabled in the future, it will be intended to help recognize the time and effort involved in development.

Support will be optional. A voluntary contribution will not grant ownership of the project or of any third-party intellectual property, and it will not include copyrighted Bloodlines game data.

No payment details are published here at this stage.
